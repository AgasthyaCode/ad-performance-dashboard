So what — the recommendation

Test a budget shift from TikTok toward Facebook

Facebook is the efficiency leader yet the smallest budget: 14% of spend, ~18% of conversions, and the lowest CPA at $7.64 — about 30% cheaper per conversion than TikTok.

TikTok is the opposite: 57% of spend and the highest CPA at $11.00, while contributing a smaller conversion share than its spend share. A directional move of ~10–15% of TikTok budget into Facebook (and Google, the proven-revenue channel at 5.6× ROAS) should lower blended CPA — worth A/B testing, not a wholesale reallocation.

One honest caveat: CPA optimization assumes every conversion is worth the same. Only Google reports revenue, so we cannot confirm TikTok's conversions aren't higher-value. And this is 30 days of one month — directional, not seasonal. Validate conversion value before reallocating heavily.
